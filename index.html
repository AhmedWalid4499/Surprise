    Last stop · Home

    Dear Mum,

    You taught us that the world is big and worth seeing, and then you went and showed it to us, one trip at a time.

    Shanghai, Milan and Lake Como, two winters in Germany, and all the ordinary days back home in Egypt. Every place was better because you were in it, laughing, planning, and taking far too many photos.

    You paint with water and colour. We tried to paint with photographs. Here's to 55 years of you, and to every journey still ahead.

    Happy birthday. With all our love,
    Ahmed & Omar
